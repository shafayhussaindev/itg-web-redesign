# Brag Plan: ITG Technologies

## What is this app?
The corporate website for ITG Technologies, an enterprise software company — a
React and Vite site whose whole navigation tree, from five top-level pages down
to twenty-seven detail pages, is generated from one editable content layer.

## The angle
This is not a joke project, so the video does not reach for one. The brag is
**architecture**: the site looks like a large hand-built marketing site, and it
is actually one system. Every card is one card. Every detail page family shares
one foundation. Fifty-one industry segments and twenty services are rows in a
content file that a non-developer can edit.

So the arc is: show the real site → open the real menu → land on a real detail
page → reveal that all of it is one system. The payoff is the ratio, and the
ratio is true.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The site's own hero line, in the site's own typeface, on the site's own navy:
**"Building Intelligent Platforms for an AI-driven World"** with the second line
in the brand's soft accent. No brag yet — establish that this is a real, serious
product before claiming anything about it.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The Industries mega menu opening.** Eleven category rows arriving one by
  one in two columns, then the flyout with five real segment names. This single
  shot is the scale claim, made visually rather than stated.
- **A tier-2 page landing.** The Healthcare & Life Sciences hero: breadcrumb,
  icon eyebrow, and the real headline "Secure by obligation. / Usable by
  necessity." Then one real segment card sliding up over it.
- **The ratio.** 27 pages / 51 segments / 1 content layer, revealed one at a
  time and then held together.

## Outro / punchline
The wordmark, and the quiet version of the claim: **"One content layer.
Twenty-seven pages."** No exclamation, no CTA. The restraint is the tone.

## User flow worth showing
Real and worth showing — it is the flow the tier-2 work unlocked:
1. **Entry** — open the Industries menu in the header.
2. **Key action** — pick a sector (Healthcare & Life Sciences).
3. **Result** — the sector's own page, with its segments.

Scenes 2 and 3 are that flow. They are the centerpiece, not the stat row.

## Tone
- Preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: a quiet engineering-craft film — the site presented the
  way the site presents itself
- Interpretation: few scenes, long holds, soft crossfades, no zoom cuts, no
  caps-lock. Confidence through restraint. The numbers land because nothing
  around them shouts.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 21.5 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#0D2140` (`--navy`), with `#EAF0F8` (`--ice`) and `#FFFFFF` for the light page surfaces
- Accent: `#3D6FB4` (`--brand-accent`) on light, `#A8C6EA` (`--brand-accent-soft`) on navy
- Text: `#FFFFFF` on navy, `#0D2140` headings and `#4A5568` (`--charcoal`) body on light
- Supporting accent: `#0D9488` (`--teal`) for ticks and small marks
- Display font: **Sora** (400/500/600/700) — the site's only display face
- Body font: **Sora**; **JetBrains Mono** (400/500) for numbers and small labels
- Strongest visual element: the tier-2 dark hero (navy scrim over photography,
  breadcrumb, icon eyebrow, two-line headline with the accent second line) and
  the two-column segment card beside it

## Share copy (draft)
Shipped the ITG Technologies site: 5 top-level pages, 27 detail pages, 51
industry segments — all generated from one content layer a non-developer can edit.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm corporate bed, low and steady, with restrained motion-matched accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (114.84 BPM)
- Music treatment: start at 0.0s, sit low (~0.22-0.28) so it never competes with
  the type, lift slightly under the stat reveal, fade out under the final wordmark
- Music cue guidance: preset read from
  `assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`.
  Strong cues to target: **3.70s** (accent line lands), **6.34s** (menu flyout
  opens), **10.54s** (tier-2 page lands). Beat grid at ~0.52s spacing — for the
  eleven menu rows use consecutive beats; for the three readable stats use
  **every other beat** (~1.05s apart) so each number can be read.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — let the navy hero's glow and the page
  card's presence breathe with music RMS. No waveform bars, no particles, no
  strobing.
- SFX posture: sparse and professional. A soft card sound per menu row is too
  much at eleven rows — use one quiet tick on the flyout, one soft impact when
  the tier-2 page lands, one per stat, and nothing else.
- Audio-coupled moments: menu rows arriving in sequence; the flyout opening; the
  page landing; the three stats ticking in.
- Restraint rule: no whoosh stack, no riser into the outro, nothing that makes a
  B2B site sound like a game trailer. If in doubt, leave it silent.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Real hero — 4.2s
Navy field (`#0D2140`) with a soft radial lift, the ITG wordmark small and top-left.
Centre: the site's verbatim hero headline, Sora 600, white, with the second line
in `#A8C6EA`:
"Building Intelligent Platforms for an" / "AI-driven World".
Sequential/interaction: none — one settled two-line headline, held ~2.6s after entry.
Audio intent: music establishes quietly; nothing else.
Audio-coupled idea: land the accent line on the 3.70s strong cue.
Music: warm, low, just started.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The menu opens — 5.3s
Recreate the header's Industries mega menu on a light surface: the nav bar strip
across the top, then the dropdown — two columns of category rows, then the
flyout panel to the right.
Real category names, in order: Enterprise & Corporate, Manufacturing & Industrial,
Retail & Consumer Goods, Logistics Supply Chain & Operations, Healthcare & Life
Sciences, Real Estate Construction & Facilities, Professional Services,
Government & Public Sector, Energy Sustainability & ESG, Education & Research,
Travel & Hospitality Services.
Flyout (Healthcare & Life Sciences): Hospitals & Healthcare Providers, Clinics &
Medical Centers, HealthTech Companies, Pharmaceuticals & Life Sciences, Medical
Supply & Distribution.
Sequential/interaction: **yes** — the eleven rows arrive one by one on consecutive
beats; the Healthcare row then highlights and the flyout slides open to its right.
Audio intent: the pulse becomes audible; the sequence feels deliberate, not busy.
Audio-coupled idea: rows on the beat grid; one quiet tick when the flyout opens
at the 6.34s strong cue.
Transition mood: clean crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The page lands — 5.0s
The Healthcare & Life Sciences tier-2 page. Dark photographic hero with the navy
scrim: breadcrumb "Home / Industries / Healthcare & Life Sciences", icon eyebrow
"HEALTHCARE & LIFE SCIENCES", then the real two-line headline
"Secure by obligation." / "Usable by necessity." in white and `#A8C6EA`.
A white segment card then rises over the lower third: "01 · Hospitals &
Healthcare Providers", "Provider operations", and two ticked focus lines
("Patient administration and scheduling", "Clinical record access and audit").
Sequential/interaction: **yes** — hero settles first, then the card rises; the
two tick lines arrive after it, one beat apart.
Audio intent: arrival. One soft impact, then back to the bed.
Audio-coupled idea: page lands on the 10.54s strong cue.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The ratio — 4.5s
Back to navy. Three figures in JetBrains Mono, large, spaced across the frame,
each with a Sora label beneath in `#A8C6EA`:
**27** "detail pages" · **51** "industry segments" · **1** "content layer".
Sequential/interaction: **yes** — one at a time, every other beat (~1.05s apart),
then all three held together for ~1.4s.
Audio intent: three small ticks, one per figure; music lifts very slightly.
Audio-coupled idea: figures on alternating beats so each reads.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Wordmark — 2.5s
Navy. ITG wordmark centred. Beneath it, one line in `#A8C6EA`:
"One content layer. Twenty-seven pages."
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: music fades out under the line; end clean, not abrupt.
Audio-coupled idea: none — let it go quiet.

**Music mood for this video:** warm corporate, low and steady
**Audio summary:** A quiet bed that becomes rhythmic exactly once — while the
menu and the stats sequence — then withdraws entirely under the closing line.
